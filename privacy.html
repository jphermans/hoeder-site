Hoeder Privacy policy

Privacy policy

The short version: Hoeder collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no server to send anything to. What you put in the vault stays on your device until you export it.

Last updated: 16 September 2026 · Applies to the Hoeder apps for iOS and Android.

What Hoeder does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address, no profile.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no usage statistics.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No crash reporter that reads your vault. Release builds contain no diagnostics that transmit anything.
  • No cloud service of ours. There is no Hoeder server holding your anchors.
  • No data sold, shared or disclosed — there is nothing to sell.

The app does not even include a third-party analytics or advertising library, which is why it needs no tracking-permission prompt.

What is stored, and where

Everything you create lives in the app's private storage on your own device: the anchors and their text, photos you attach, voice notes you record, and your settings. That folder is private to Hoeder; other apps cannot read it, and on iOS it is excluded from the app's shared containers.

How it is protected

  • Free text is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, under keys derived for that purpose, before it is written to the database.
  • The vault key is random and never stored in the open. It is wrapped with a key derived from your six-digit PIN using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, and the wrapped result is kept in the operating system's keychain (iOS keychain, Android keystore). It never leaves the device.
  • Unlocking is local. A PIN is verified by decrypting the wrapped key on the device; nothing is sent anywhere to check it. Biometric unlock, if you enable it, stores a second copy of that key behind an OS-enforced access control.
  • Photos and voice notes are files inside the app's private storage, protected by the operating system's file protection, and are not additionally encrypted on top of that. Anyone who can unlock your device and reach the app's storage can read them.
  • Deleted means deleted on this device: deleting the vault removes the database, the media and the key material.

The honest limits: a six-digit PIN is short, and device-level attacks — a jailbroken or rooted device, malware with your device unlocked, or someone who knows your PIN — are outside what any app-side encryption can promise.

What leaves your device

  • Exports you make yourself. Settings offers a vault archive and a printable report. Where that file goes is entirely your choice: a folder on the device, a share sheet target, or a folder your device syncs. Encrypted archives stay encrypted; anything else you choose to share is your decision, not ours.
  • Purchases. If you subscribe to Hoeder Pro, the transaction is handled by Apple or Google. We receive no name, no address, no email and no payment details — only the anonymous, store-issued information needed to know whether the subscription is active. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies govern that processing.

Permissions, and why they exist

  • Camera and photo library — only when you attach a photo to an anchor, and only the item you pick.
  • Microphone — only while you record a voice note, capped at 30 seconds.
  • Face ID or fingerprint — only if you turn on biometric unlock, and only to read the key from the keychain.
  • Files / document picker — only when you choose where to write an export or which file to import.

Refusing any of them leaves the rest of the app working. Nothing is requested in the background.

Children

Hoeder is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected, there is no database of yours for us to look into, export or erase. In practice, the rights you would exercise are already in your hands:

  • Access and portability — export the vault at any time; it is a documented, versioned archive.
  • Erasure — delete the vault in Settings › Security, or uninstall the app.
  • Objection or restriction — there is no processing to object to.

If you are in the EU or UK and believe something here is wrong, you have the right to complain to your national data protection authority; in Belgium that is the Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données.

Changes

If a future version of Hoeder were to collect anything, this page would say so before that version shipped, and the change would be described here rather than buried. The date at the top is when this text last changed.
