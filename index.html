Hoeder A vault for the few things that must survive you.

Support

Hoeder is a life-stewardship vault for iOS and Android: a small number of anchors — a person, an object, a place, a skill, a promise — each with why it matters, what keeps it alive, and who receives it. It is local-first: no account, no feed, no ads, and no server of ours.

Getting help

Write to @jphermans on GitHub and describe what happened. Please include the version from Settings › About, your iOS or Android version, and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots help.

This is a small independent app, not a company with a support desk. Reports are read and answered as time allows.

The app speaks Dutch, French and English; support is answered in Dutch or English.

Questions people actually ask

I forgot my PIN. Can you reset it?
No, and that is deliberate: the vault key is wrapped with a key derived from your PIN, and neither is stored anywhere in a readable form. There is no reset, no backdoor and no recovery service. If you exported a vault file earlier, that file — hoeder-vault-<date>.hoeder, sealed with the passphrase you chose — is the way back in, on any phone.
Where is my data?
In the app's private storage on your own device, plus any vault file you chose to export. Free text is encrypted; photos and voice notes sit in the app's private folder, protected by the operating system. Nothing is uploaded to us — we have nowhere to upload it to.
What happens if I delete the app?
It takes the vault with it. That is the honest trade of local-first, which is why setup will not let you create your first anchor before you have chosen a backup path and acknowledged that. Export first, or keep the vault in a folder your device backs up.
How do I delete everything?
Settings › Security › Delete the vault removes the database, the media and the key material from this device. Uninstalling the app does the same. Exported files are yours to delete wherever you put them.
How do I cancel Hoeder Pro?
Subscriptions belong to the store that sold them, so they are cancelled there: on iOS, Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions; on Android, Play Store › Menu › Subscriptions. Restoring a purchase on a new device uses Restore on the Pro screen. Everything except the yearly ritual and the printable report is free without a subscription.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no account, no sign-in and no email address on file.

Privacy

Hoeder collects nothing: no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting that reads your vault, and no server of ours. Your anchors never leave the device unless you export them yourself. The full text is in the privacy policy.
